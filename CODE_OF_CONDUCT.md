# Code of Conduct

Author: Viquar Khan.

This project follows the spirit of the Apache Software Foundation Code of Conduct.

## Our standards

We are here to build useful software together. That works when everyone can participate without
having to spend energy on anything other than the work.

Expected behaviour:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Most disagreements are about context, not competence.
- Be direct about code and gentle about people. Review the change, not the author.
- Be open to being wrong, and say so plainly when you are.
- Give credit generously and ask for help early.
- Respect that contributors work across time zones, first languages and experience levels.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form.
- Personal or political attacks, insults or demeaning comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or issues.
- Any conduct that a reasonable professional would consider inappropriate in a workplace.

## Scope

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages,
documentation, and any public setting where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the project maintainer through
<https://github.com/vaquarkhan>. Reports are handled confidentially. Include what happened, where,
and when, plus links if the behaviour is on record.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences decided by
the other maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will respond in proportion to the behaviour. Depending on severity and history, a
response may be a private correction, a public warning, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a
permanent ban. The reporter is told the outcome as far as confidentiality allows.
