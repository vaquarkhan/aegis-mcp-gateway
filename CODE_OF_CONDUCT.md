# Code of Conduct

Aegis is an Apache Incubator project and follows the [ASF Code of Conduct](https://www.apache.org/foundation/policies/conduct.html).
This document records the project-facing standards and supplemental repository scope. Where it conflicts
with applicable ASF policy or process, the ASF policy or process controls.

## Our standards

We build software together. Participation should be professional, respectful, and open to people with
different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

Expected behaviour:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Most disagreements are about context, not competence.
- Review the change, not the author.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct them.
- Respect contributors' time zones, first languages, and experience levels.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal or political attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or issues.
- Conduct a reasonable professional would consider inappropriate in an open-source project.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, documentation, and
any public setting where a person represents the project.

## Reporting

Do not post a conduct report or personal details in a public issue. Follow the reporting and escalation
instructions in the [ASF Code of Conduct](https://www.apache.org/foundation/policies/conduct.html).
A project-specific private contact may supplement that route when it is published, but it does not
replace ASF reporting or enforcement processes.

## Enforcement

Conduct reports are handled under the applicable ASF Code of Conduct process. Repository maintainers
must not make a project-local decision that conflicts with that process. A maintainer who is directly
involved in a report, or whose impartiality could reasonably be questioned, must recuse from any
project-specific support role.
